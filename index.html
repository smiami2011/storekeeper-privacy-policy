Privacy Policy for Storekeeper

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Storekeeper ("the App") is an inventory and store management application designed to help users manage items, receipts, supplies, stock balances, costing and related reports.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Storekeeper collects, how that information is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Storekeeper, the App may process the following information:

  • Account information: Email address and authentication-related information required to create and maintain your Storekeeper account.
  • Profile information: Information that you voluntarily provide in your Storekeeper profile, such as your profile name.
  • Store and inventory information: Information entered by you, such as item names, quantities, received quantities, supply quantities, dates, invoice information, opening balances, and related inventory records.
  • Trial and licence information: Information necessary to manage free-trial eligibility and account/licence status.
  • Locally stored application data: Store and inventory information may be stored locally on your device to allow the App to operate and provide its inventory features.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Storekeeper is used only as necessary to provide and maintain the App and its features.

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To maintain your profile.
  • To provide inventory and store management features.
  • To calculate stock balances and moving-average costing.
  • To generate reports and related records.
  • To manage free-trial eligibility and licence status.
  • To maintain application security and prevent misuse.
  • To respond to support requests.

3. Firebase Services

Storekeeper uses Firebase services provided by Google to support account authentication and certain cloud-based application data.

Firebase Authentication is used to create and authenticate Storekeeper accounts. Certain account and trial-related information may be stored using Cloud Firestore.

Firebase services process information according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

4. Local Storage

Storekeeper uses local storage on your device, including a local database, to store inventory and store-management information required for the App's functionality.

Local data is associated with the Storekeeper account being used on the device. The App is designed so that data belonging to different accounts on the same device is kept separately.

5. Data Sharing

Storekeeper does not sell your personal information.

We do not share your inventory records with advertisers or sell your inventory information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the App, such as Firebase services used for authentication and cloud data storage.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of the App.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Storekeeper does not currently use third-party advertising services or third-party analytics services to serve advertisements or track users for advertising purposes.

7. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Storekeeper account through the account deletion feature provided in the App.

When an account is deleted, the App removes the account from the authentication system and removes the user's locally stored application data associated with that account, subject to applicable technical, legal, security, and fraud-prevention requirements.

Certain limited records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or to prevent abuse of free-trial eligibility. Such records are not used to provide normal application functionality after account deletion.

8. Free Trial and Trial Eligibility

Storekeeper may provide a limited free-trial period to eligible users.

Deleting an account does not reset or restore free-trial eligibility. This measure is used to prevent repeated creation and deletion of accounts for the purpose of obtaining additional free-trial periods.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by Storekeeper against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Storekeeper is intended for general users and is not specifically directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Storekeeper, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this webpage with a revised "Last updated" date.
