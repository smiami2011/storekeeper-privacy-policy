Delete Your Storekeeper Account

This page provides an external way to request deletion of your Storekeeper account and associated data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you are unable to use the Storekeeper app, or have already uninstalled it, you can request account deletion by contacting us using the email address below.

Send your account deletion request to:

lifeinmotion.offc@gmail.com

Please use the email address associated with your Storekeeper account when sending the request.

What Will Be Deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete the Storekeeper account and the data associated with that account that is eligible for deletion, including account-related information and locally stored Storekeeper data associated with the account.

Data Retention

Certain limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, free-trial abuse prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate purposes. Any such retention is handled in accordance with the Storekeeper Privacy Policy.
